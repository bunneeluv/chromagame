How to Play Chroma

Chroma is a color-memory game played over 5 rounds.

  1. You’ll see a color inside the frame for 10 seconds.
  2. Memorize the color.
  3. The frame turns white and CMYK sliders appear.
  4. Recreate the color as closely as possible.
  5. Your score is based on how close your guess is to the original.

After 5 rounds, your scores are averaged and converted into a school letter grade (A–F).
